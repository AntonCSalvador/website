The most rewarding part of building this platform is seeing colleagues use it around the office. As its technical owner, lead engineer, and founding engineer, I work on the engineering that turns an AI tool into something people can use in production.

## AI that fits into real work

The platform brings AI into internal workflows. My contribution spans the core platform and the safeguards around it: high-level access controls, observability, and human-in-the-loop components.

Role-based access control helps keep capabilities appropriate to each person's role. Observability with LangSmith makes the behavior of AI workflows visible. Human-in-the-loop components make room for a person to participate where the workflow needs one.

## Built for production

The platform is in production at Dun & Bradstreet, following the company's required processes. Its AI workflows use LangChain and LangGraph, with LangSmith supporting observability.

Watching something I built become part of a colleague's day is the result that matters most to me.
